MINESWEEPER ON A WALL · PLAYED WITH PHONES
A TEASER FOR NEWLINE 2027

WHAT THIS IS

A minesweeper the size of a wall. Your phone is the controller.

The board is projected on the wall in front of you. Every hidden tile is a QR code. Scan one, pick what to do with it, and watch it happen on the wall. Everyone in the room plays the same board at the same time.

HOW TO PLAY

1
SCAN A TILE

Point your phone camera at any QR code on the wall. No app, no account. The link opens in your browser.

2
REVEAL OR FLAG

The page shows the tile you scanned and two buttons. REVEAL opens it. FLAG marks it as a mine.

3
LOOK AT THE WALL

The tile changes on the wall within a second. Scan the next one. Clear every safe tile together, or find out where the mines are.

RULES

Minesweeper, as you know it

  • QRA QR code is a hidden tile. Scan it to act on it.
  • 3A number is how many mines touch that tile, including diagonals.
  • An empty tile has no mines around it. Its neighbours open by themselves.
  • A hollow square is a flag. Scan a flagged tile again to remove it.
  • Revealing a mine ends the round for everyone. The board shows where the mines were, then a new game starts.
  • The first reveal of a round is always safe. Reveal every safe tile to win.
